## 01 --- Register / Login

Split editorial authentication experience: - food visual - warm cream
form panel - WELCOME BACK - login/register toggle - email/password -
recovery/social options - orange CTA

## 02 --- Login Entry Animation

Single 3--4 second cinematic transition: LOGIN SUCCESS → HANG TIGHT →
chilli burst → ingredient vortex → SpicyEat logo → LET'S EAT → Home.

## 03 --- Home / Landing

Hero: SPICY. / CRISPY. / GOOD.

Sections: hero → ingredient story → signature product → categories →
spice experience → best sellers → brand story → CTA → interactive chilli
footer.

## 04 --- Explore

Hero: WHAT'S YOUR CRAVING?

Supporting: Pick a mood. We'll handle the hunger.

Use visual categories, featured foods, popular dishes, spice selector
and editorial promotional sections.

## 05 --- Menu

Food-first navigation: ALL / BURGERS / FRIED CHICKEN / PIZZA / WRAPS &
ROLLS / LOADED / PASTA / SIDES / DESSERTS.

Use editorial food compositions, spice indicators, price and MAKE IT
MINE.

## 06 --- Menu Item

Example: FIRE SMASH BURGER

Copy: BUILT TO DRIP.

Include image gallery, description, spice level, price, quantity,
customisation, add-ons and related foods.

## 07 --- Cart

Headline: YOUR CART. / GOOD CHOICES.

Include item controls, savings, delivery progress, summary, add-ons and
LET'S EAT.

## 08 --- Search

Headline: FIND YOUR CRAVING.

Include prominent search, suggestions, filters, spice/veg/price
controls, results and sorting.

Search food/menu items, never restaurants.

## 09 --- Collections / Offers / Rewards

Collections: Fire Mode, Late Night Cravings, Cheese Pull, Chicken
Lovers, Under ₹299, Best Sellers.

Offers: 20% OFF, FREE DELIVERY, COMBO DEALS, FIRST ORDER, WEEKEND HEAT.

Rewards: Spicy Coins, balance, progress, earning history and redeemable
rewards.

## 10 --- Checkout

Two-column checkout: address, delivery, contact, payment, coupon,
special instructions + order summary, savings, fees and total.

CTA: PLACE ORDER

## 11 --- Order Confirmed

Headline: ORDER CONFIRMED!

Show order ID, items, total, payment, ETA and delivery info.

CTA: VIEW ORDER

## 12 --- Order Details / Tracking

Statuses: PREPARING / ON THE WAY / ALMOST THERE

Timeline: PLACED → CONFIRMED → PREPARING → READY → OUT FOR DELIVERY →
DELIVERED

Include items, total, address, payment, delivery partner, ETA,
map/tracking, contact/help and reorder.

## 13 --- Profile

Headline: HEY, FOODIE.

Include profile, Spicy Coins, favourites, order history, addresses,
payments, preferences, security and logout.

## Reference handling

-   Recreate the visual hierarchy as real responsive UI.
-   Do not put the reference screenshots inside the app.
-   Do not copy proprietary Sunbeam assets or source code.
-   Keep SpicyEat copy and imagery original.
-   Preserve the editorial, playful, premium and food-focused visual
    language.
